Works with direct audio sources

TactiBeat works with local audio and video files, microphone input, and direct audio stream URLs. Protected music-service streams are not supported. Vibration cannot preserve pitch, lyrics, or timbre, so the app focuses on timing, pulse, bass weight, and rhythm feel.

FAQ

Is TactiBeat a medical device?

No. It is not a medical device and does not replace hearing aids, cochlear implants, clinical care, or professional advice.

Who is it for?

It can be useful for accessibility experiments, deaf and hard-of-hearing listeners, tactile audio research, and rhythm exploration.
