A little context before you press play.

Haptics require a compatible iPhone. Start with a built-in demo and Test Haptic to check how it feels on your device.

Real-time haptics work with compatible direct audio and video URLs. Some sources play without exposing decoded audio; the app then shows that live analysis is unavailable. HLS streams currently support playback only, without live haptic analysis. Protected music-service streams are not supported.

TactiBeat is not a medical device and does not replace hearing aids, cochlear implants, clinical care, or professional advice.
