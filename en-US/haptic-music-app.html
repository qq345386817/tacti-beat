Works with direct audio sources

TactiBeat works with local audio and video files, microphone input, and direct audio stream URLs. Protected music-service streams are not supported. Vibration cannot preserve pitch, lyrics, or timbre, so the app focuses on timing, pulse, bass weight, and rhythm feel.

FAQ

Does it work with any song?

It works best with percussion, downbeats, bass pulses, pauses, and clear energy changes.

Can it reproduce melody?

No. Haptics are better for timing and pulse than pitch, lyrics, or timbre.

TactiBeat is not a medical device and does not replace hearing aids, cochlear implants, clinical care, or professional advice.
