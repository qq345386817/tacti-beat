Live audio haptics

Use the microphone or direct audio streams for real-time haptic activity.

Best with rhythm-rich audio

The app works best with percussion, downbeats, rests, and strong energy changes. Streaming is for direct audio stream URLs; protected music-service streams are not supported. Vibration cannot faithfully preserve pitch, lyrics, or timbre, so TactiBeat focuses on timing, pulse, and rhythmic feel.
