TactiBeat Help

Start with a built-in demo, choose the right sound source, and tune the haptics on a compatible iPhone.

Download on the App Store

Try your first tactile beat

  1. Open one of the four built-in demos. Its audio is included with the app; there is no track download to wait for.
  2. Tap Analyze in the demo, then Play + Haptics. Imported local audio and video files start analysis automatically after preparation.
  3. Open Settings from the file, Live Sound or stream page. Compare the six mapping presets and adjust intensity, sensitivity, density or timing offset.
  4. Saved results keep the settings used to create them. To apply a different feel, choose Regenerate, review the settings and save to analyze again.

Your sound. Three ways to feel it.

  • Audio & video files Import a file or try a built-in demo. Analyze once, save the result, and replay the rhythm with synchronized haptics.
  • Live Sound Let the microphone pick up nearby music and sound, and follow its energy through real-time tactile pulses.
  • Streams Play a compatible direct audio or video URL and feel its rhythm as it plays. HLS supports playback only.

Compare the same demo with different presets. Feel Check summarizes strength, rhythm and edge; Test Haptic checks device output. Pocket Mode simplifies the Live Sound screen, but does not promise background or lock-screen listening.

Why do I feel separate pulses instead of constant vibration?

  • TactiBeat is designed around rhythm-first pulses. Clear gaps make beats easier to read through touch.
  • Dense audio may be simplified so the tactile pattern does not collapse into a continuous buzz.

What music works best?

  • Tracks with percussion, strong downbeats, rests, and clear energy changes usually feel best.
  • Ambient pads, vocals-only material, or very dense mixes may produce less distinct tactile rhythm.

Troubleshooting

  • If pulses feel too dense, lower density or sensitivity.
  • If pulses feel too weak, raise intensity or try a sharper haptic style.
  • If timing feels offset, adjust timing offset until the pulse aligns with the sound.

Questions before you press play

Can I try TactiBeat without downloading a track?

Yes. Four CC0 demo tracks are bundled with the app. Open a demo, tap Analyze and play its generated haptics on a compatible iPhone. The demo audio does not need a separate download.

Which audio sources support haptics?

Analyze complete local audio or video files and replay saved results offline. Live Sound uses the microphone with permission. Compatible direct audio and video URLs can drive haptics while playing and need a network connection. HLS supports playback only, without real-time haptic analysis; protected music-service streams are not supported.

Why do I hear audio but feel no vibration?

Haptic output requires an iPhone with supported haptic hardware. Use Test Haptic to check output. In Live Sound, allow microphone access, move closer to the sound and adjust sensitivity if the input is too quiet. iPad layouts do not imply equivalent vibration hardware. The effect varies with the device, material and settings.

Does TactiBeat capture audio inside other apps?

No. Live Sound listens to nearby sound through the microphone; it is not system-audio capture. Use your own files or a compatible direct media URL for playback inside TactiBeat.

Do new settings change my saved haptic results?

No. Saved analyses retain their own settings. Open Settings from an analysis, Live Sound or stream page to change defaults. For an existing file result, choose Regenerate and save the reviewed settings to generate a new analysis.

Can vibration reproduce melody or improve hearing?

Phone vibration cannot faithfully preserve pitch, lyrics or timbre. TactiBeat focuses on timing, pulse and rhythmic feel. It is not a medical device and does not replace hearing aids, cochlear implants, clinical care or professional advice.
