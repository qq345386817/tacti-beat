## Generation Prompt

This is a CONCEPTUAL BRAND ILLUSTRATION, NOT an app screenshot, not a chart UI, and not evidence of measured audio. Create a beautiful, materially convincing, high-end 3D editorial sound-to-touch sculpture. A single cohesive horizontal composition: on the left a flowing cyan acoustic ribbon has a clear rolling waveform silhouette; through the middle it resolves into several crisp violet beat fins; toward the right the fins become distinctly separated tactile pulse forms rising from a slender translucent flexible ribbon, conveying physical touch with small local pressure ripples in that ribbon. Keep all elements logically connected to this one sound-to-touch transition, with deliberate rhythm, pauses, and a strong large-scale silhouette. Use polished translucent glass with a few matte ceramic faces, restrained reflections, crisp edges, believable gentle contact shadows, sophisticated studio lighting. Exact core palette: violet #6d4aff, electric blue #419fe2, pulse cyan #12b3d6, deep ink #070a2d; a small amount of energetic pink is permitted to echo the existing App icon. It must have strong chromatic and material contrast, rather than a flat monochrome blue chart. Wide horizontal hero composition, approximately 3:1, complete subject inside the image with safe breathing space at every edge, no accidental clipping. All the space around the object is a genuinely transparent alpha background so this illustration can sit on either white or charcoal websites; NO opaque backplate, white floor, checkerboard pattern, giant gradient background, mist, bokeh, glow orbs, scattered balls, random confetti, unrelated decorations, or decorative card frames. Make the sculpture sufficiently substantial and tactile to carry a website first viewport at 800 pixels wide. NO text, letters, captions, numbers, logos, phones, device mockups, hands, people, UI panels, buttons, music-player controls, or medical claims. Deliver a visually striking yet coherent finished illustration, not a wireframe or placeholder.
