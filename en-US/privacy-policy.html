TactiBeat Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

Overview

TactiBeat analyzes imported audio and video audio locally to generate waveform previews and haptic cues.

Information we collect

No account is required. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or profile information to run.

Audio and video files

Imported audio and extracted video audio are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers for analysis.

Generated haptic data

The app may store waveform previews, haptic cues, timing settings, and style settings locally so playback is faster.

Demo downloads

Optional demo tracks may be downloaded from their source hosts. Those hosts and your network provider may receive standard web request information.

Analytics and tracking

TactiBeat does not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising trackers.

Contact

If you email support, we receive the address and message content you choose to send and use it to respond.

Medical disclaimer

TactiBeat is not a medical device and does not replace hearing aids, cochlear implants, clinical care, or professional advice.
