What TactiBeat does

Rhythm-first pulses

Prioritizes clear tactile hits with readable gaps instead of turning every loud moment into continuous vibration.

Local analysis

Import audio or extract audio from video, then analyze waveform, rhythm peaks, and haptic cues on device.

Tunable feel

Adjust intensity, sensitivity, density, timing offset, and haptic style to make pulses easier to read.

Best with rhythm-rich audio

The app works best with percussion, downbeats, rests, and strong energy changes. Vibration cannot faithfully preserve pitch, lyrics, or timbre, so TactiBeat focuses on timing, pulse, and rhythmic feel.
