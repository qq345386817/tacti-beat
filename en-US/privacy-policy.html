TactiBeat Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Download on the App Store

Overview

TactiBeat analyzes imported audio and video audio locally to generate waveform previews and haptic cues.

Information we collect

No account is required. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or profile information to run.

Audio and video files

Imported audio and extracted video audio are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers for analysis.

Generated haptic data

The app may store waveform previews, haptic cues, timing settings, and style settings locally so playback is faster.

Bundled demos

Four demo tracks are included with the app and do not require a network download. Their attribution links open the source website only when you choose to visit them.

Anonymous product analytics

TactiBeat sends first-party anonymous product interaction and reliability events, such as app launches and whether import, analysis, playback, Live Sound, or Stream actions succeed or fail. The data may include random installation and session identifiers, app and build versions, OS version, locale, source category, and duration bucket. Installation and session identifiers are HMAC-hashed before database storage. The event database does not contain imported media, audio, microphone recordings, file names, source URLs, free-form text, account details, or IP addresses. Cloudflare provides the endpoint and database infrastructure and processes standard network request information to deliver and protect the service. This data is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Contact

If you email support, we receive the address and message content you choose to send and use it to respond.

Medical disclaimer

TactiBeat is not a medical device and does not replace hearing aids, cochlear implants, clinical care, or professional advice.
